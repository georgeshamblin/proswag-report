02 · Why Google cannot file you as workwear

The site says retail. Google believes it.

We crawled all 327 pages in the sitemap and ran Lighthouse on the homepage and the hat collection, 24 September 2026.

Critical

No page for the business you are in

The store has 38 collections: fishing, golf, camo, youth, dog collars, a July 4 sale. None for company polos, crew shirts, custom hats or a corporate program. 0 of 327 page titles say "embroidery" or "embroidered", and embroidery is free on every order.

Critical

The one corporate page is a form

/pages/contractor-pricing-request is the only page written for a company buyer: 240 words and no H1 heading. The customization page has a better story (958 words) under the heading "Your brand. Stitched in.", which names no product or buyer.

Critical

The homepage has an empty H1

The page title says "Custom Apparel, Promotional Products & Gifts". The main heading, the strongest signal on the page, is blank. Google falls back on the product grid, which is fishing and golf.

High

14.0 seconds to load on a phone

Lighthouse mobile: 55/100 on the homepage (6.6 MB, 240 requests), 60/100 on the hat collection (10.7 s). Google uses the phone version of the site to rank it.

High

Leftovers from the old site are still indexed

/pages/cart, /pages/checkout and /pages/my-account are in the sitemap with the description "[woocommerce_cart]". Also live: /pages/test-this, two contact pages, an About page (/pages/about-us-2) whose description is Latin placeholder text, and /pages/landing-page, a 404 that AI engines still cite 13 times.

High

Your best proof is a picture

18 customer logos, Heritage Roofing, ABC Supply, Owens Corning, SRS, Epiroc and Harley-Davidson among them, are images. No page says in words who ProSwag outfits. For a corporate buyer that list is the whole pitch.

Medium

No address, phone or business listing on the site

The Organization schema has a name and a logo only. No page lists 205 Mercy Way or a phone number. Without them ProSwag cannot show in the map pack, and AI engines fill the gap with numbers from data brokers.

Medium

Thin, repeated product copy

12 polo pages share one description. 31 product titles are cut off mid-word in Google ("Sable Bla"). Collection descriptions read "Camo" and "Gear". The blog has no posts and five trashed URLs in the sitemap.

Working

What already works

Lighthouse SEO score 100/100. Every page returns clean to Google and to AI crawlers. 271 products are indexed and the retail collections already reach pages two and three, so Google trusts the domain. The work is pointing that trust at the corporate side.

What moves first, and why

Weeks 1 to 4. The by-name results. Once the H1, titles, address and turnaround agree, Google and the AI engines describe ProSwag the way you do, and the "verify before ordering" caveat has nothing to point at.

Weeks 4 to 8. Headwear and local. "Custom trucker hats" and "richardson custom hats" have low difficulty scores, and the hat catalog is your deepest (96 hats). Local embroidery searches have the fewest competitors.

Weeks 8 to 12 and after. The corporate terms. "Custom corporate apparel" is the hardest term on the list. Expect top 50 by day 90 and page one after that, as the industry pages and customer stories build up.

What a ranking is worth here

A company that finds ProSwag through "custom company polos" is not buying one shirt. It is buying a crew order, then the reorder, then the company store. Google advertisers pay up to $14.86 a click for these searches today. Each page that ranks earns that click every month without the ad spend.

It also feeds the Nativz creator program. Creator content gives the engines more to cite, and a site that ranks for workwear gives that paid traffic somewhere to land that looks like the brand you described.
